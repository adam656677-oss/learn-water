Built for Mississippi

Made to be trusted

Operators, parents, and agencies can see exactly what the material is based on, what has changed, and where their data goes (nowhere).

Follows the state manual

Every adult lesson tracks the Mississippi Waterworks Operators Manual chapter by chapter, from Class D through Class A. The practice questions, math drills, and flash cards are drawn from that same manual.

Source: MSDH Waterworks Operators Manual

Current where the rules changed

Where federal rules have moved on since the manual, each lesson says so and gives the date: the Lead and Copper Rule Improvements, the PFAS limits, UCMR 5 and 6, and the public notice tiers, each tied to the EPA rule it comes from.

U.S. EPA · status as of September 2026

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, and no tracking scripts. Progress, badges, and Greg's chat stay in the browser. When an operator asks Greg to search EPA, NRWA, or MsRWA, only the search words go to that site.

Runs entirely in your browser

Why it matters

Water education for everyone

Water is our most essential resource, yet most people know very little about where it comes from, how it's treated, or the pipes, pumps, and people that deliver it every day.

LearnWater is a free resource created alongside the WaterOps app. It's built to help kids get curious, help communities understand their water system, and help operators earn and keep their licenses.

Start learning free

What's inside

  • 📖14 study lessons that follow the Mississippi Waterworks Operators Manual, with notes wherever current federal rules have changed
  • 🧮Operator math drills with fresh numbers every time and step-by-step solutions
  • 🗺️Deep dives on water regulations, Mississippi's 16 aquifers, and hydraulic modeling, each with interactive tools
  • 🎮Kids games like Build-a-Filter, Water Plant Tycoon, and Drop's Journey
  • 🔤Glossaries for every level: nearly 500 operator terms, and a kids' Word Lab with 150+ water words
  • 💬Greg, a built-in guide who answers questions right in your browser. Operators can also ask him to search EPA, NRWA, and MsRWA.
