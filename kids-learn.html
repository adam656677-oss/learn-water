🌍 The Water Cycle

Water never stops moving. Tap the numbers on the picture to follow water on its endless journey!

👆 Tap a step to explore

🦕 Mind-blowing thought

Because water keeps cycling forever, the water in your glass has been around for billions of years. Some of it might have fallen as rain on a dinosaur! There's no such thing as brand-new water.

⭐ Amazing Water Facts

Tap a card to flip it over and reveal the fact hiding underneath!

🧊

Ice is a weirdo

Almost everything gets denser when it freezes — but water gets LESS dense. That's why ice floats, and why fish can survive under a frozen pond.

🫧

Water sticks to itself

Water molecules grip each other so tightly they make “surface tension” — strong enough for some insects to walk right across a pond.

🌡️

Water holds heat

Water warms up and cools down slowly. That's why oceans keep coastal towns milder, and a pool stays cool on a hot morning.

🚀

Water is in space

Scientists have found frozen water on Mars and the Moon, oceans hidden under the ice of moons like Europa and Enceladus, and ice inside comets.

🧪 Try It Yourself: Real Experiments

Grab a grown-up and some kitchen supplies. When you finish one, tap We did it! to earn your Experimenter badge.

🛍️

Water Cycle in a Bag

You need: a zip-top bag, water, blue food coloring, tape, a sunny window.

  1. Pour about a quarter cup of water into the bag and add a drop of food coloring.
  2. Seal it tight and draw a sun, clouds, and waves on the bag with a marker.
  3. Tape it to a sunny window and wait a few hours.
  4. Watch droplets form at the top and “rain” back down — the whole water cycle!
🧫

Build a Water Filter

You need: a plastic bottle, coffee filter or cotton, charcoal, sand, gravel, muddy water.

  1. Have a grown-up cut the bottom off the bottle and flip it upside down.
  2. From the neck up, layer: cotton, charcoal, sand, then gravel on top.
  3. Slowly pour muddy water in and catch it in a cup below.
  4. Compare before and after! Don't drink it — filters remove dirt, not all germs.
🌸

Color-Changing Flowers

You need: white carnations or celery, cups, water, food coloring.

  1. Fill cups with water and add lots of food coloring to each.
  2. Put a white flower or celery stalk in each cup.
  3. Check back every few hours for two days.
  4. The petals change color — you're watching plants drink and transpire!
🪙

How Many Drops on a Penny?

You need: a penny, an eyedropper or straw, water, dish soap.

  1. Guess how many drops will fit on a penny. Write your guess down.
  2. Add drops one at a time and count until the water spills.
  3. Most people are shocked — it's often 20 or more!
  4. Try soapy water. Fewer drops fit, because soap breaks surface tension.
☀️

Sun vs. Shade Race

You need: two identical cups, water, a marker.

  1. Fill both cups with the same amount of water and mark the level.
  2. Put one in bright sun and one in the shade.
  3. Check the levels after a full day.
  4. The sunny cup lost more — that's evaporation powered by the sun!
🕵️

Home Water Detective

You need: paper, a pencil, food coloring, and sharp eyes.

  1. Walk through your home and list every place water is used.
  2. Check faucets for drips. Put a few drops of food coloring in the toilet tank — if color shows up in the bowl within 10 minutes without flushing, it's leaking!
  3. Time your showers for one week.
  4. Pick ONE water-saving change and track how it goes.

⚠️ Safety first, scientist!

Always have a grown-up help with cutting, hot water, or anything glass. Never drink water from an experiment — even if it looks clean, filtering doesn't remove germs.

📖 Words About Water

Water shows up all through the Bible — as a gift, a promise, and a picture of life itself.

🌊

“Come, everyone who thirsts, come to the waters...”

Isaiah 55:1 (ESV)

Just like a cold drink on a hot day, God invites everyone to come and be filled up.

💧

“...whoever drinks of the water that I will give him will never be thirsty again.”

John 4:14 (ESV)

Jesus talked about a special kind of “water” that fills your heart forever — not just your cup!

🐑

“He makes me lie down in green pastures. He leads me beside still waters.”

Psalm 23:2 (ESV)

A calm, quiet river or pond can remind us that we're cared for and safe.

🌍

“And God said, ‘Let the waters under the heavens be gathered together into one place, and let the dry land appear.’”

Genesis 1:9 (ESV)

On Day 3 of creation, God made oceans, lakes, and rivers — water has been part of the plan from the very beginning!

✨

“Then the angel showed me the river of the water of life, bright as crystal, flowing from the throne of God and of the Lamb.”

Revelation 22:1 (ESV)

The Bible ends with a picture of the cleanest, brightest river ever — sparkling like crystal!

⛰️

“...and water shall come out of it, and the people will drink.”

Exodus 17:6 (ESV)

When people in the desert were thirsty, God provided water right out of a rock — a reminder that water is a gift, not something to waste.

🌱

“He is like a tree planted by streams of water that yields its fruit in its season, and its leaf does not wither.”

Psalm 1:3 (ESV)

Plants near water grow strong and healthy — a picture of what happens when we stay close to good things.

🕊️

“...the waters receded from the earth continually.”

Genesis 8:3 (ESV)

After the flood, the waters went back down and life started fresh — water can reshape the whole world.
