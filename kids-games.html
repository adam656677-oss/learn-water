    🔎 What you're learning

    Real treatment plants filter water through layers of sand, gravel, and sometimes carbon. Filters catch dirt — but germs are too tiny to see, so water is always disinfected afterward. Try this for real in the Discovery Lab (with a grown-up), but never drink your experiment water!

      🔎 What you're learning

      Real operators balance these same choices every day: enough coagulant for the cloudiness, backwashing filters before they clog, and just the right chlorine dose. Cloudy water shields germs from chlorine — that's why clearing the water comes first!

      🔎 What you're learning

      A faucet dripping once a second can waste more than 3,000 gallons a year, and a running toilet can waste 200 gallons a day. Finding and fixing leaks is one of the biggest ways to save water at home.

      🔎 The five big steps

      Screening catches big stuff · Coagulation makes tiny dirt clump into floc · Settling lets heavy floc sink · Filtration strains out tiny leftovers · Disinfection kills germs with chlorine or UV light.
