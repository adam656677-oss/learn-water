🔎 What you're learning

A single dripping faucet can waste more than 3,000 gallons a year — and a running toilet can waste 200 gallons a day. Finding and fixing leaks fast is one of the biggest ways to save water at home.

🔎 The four big steps

Screening catches big stuff like sticks and leaves · Settling lets heavy dirt sink to the bottom · Filtration strains out tiny particles through sand and carbon · Disinfection uses chlorine or UV light to kill germs.

🔎 What you're learning

The water cycle never stops. Sun heats water into vapor, vapor cools into clouds, clouds drop rain and snow, water collects in oceans and underground — and plants push water back into the air too. Same water, over and over, forever.
